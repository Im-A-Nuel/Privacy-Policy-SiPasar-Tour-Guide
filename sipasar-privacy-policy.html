Privacy Policy

JelajahDW is a navigation and tour guide app for traditional markets in Yogyakarta. This policy explains what data we handle, why we handle it, and your choices.

Last updated 22 July 2026 UKDW & BAPEDA : Indonesia

App Preview

Scope

This policy covers data processed by the JelajahDW mobile app to provide routing, real-time navigation, simulation mode, and contextual tour guidance (text and optional audio).

Data We Handle

We collect only what is necessary to power navigation and keep the app reliable.

Location (precise or approximate)

  • Purpose: show maps, compute routes, and enable real-time navigation from/to markets and nearby POIs.
  • Collection: through OS location services (via Google Play services location) when you grant permission.
  • Processing & Storage: used on device and in live requests to our map/navigation provider, Mapbox; we do not persist your real-time location on our own servers.
  • Sharing: not sold; your location is transmitted to Mapbox strictly to render maps and compute routes, under Mapbox's own privacy policy.
  • Control: you can allow, limit to "approximate", or revoke location in device Settings. Simulation mode works without location.

Diagnostics and Crash Logs

  • Purpose: improve stability and performance.
  • Collection: we do not use any third-party crash reporting or analytics SDK (e.g. Firebase Crashlytics) in the app. Crash and ANR (App Not Responding) reports may still be collected automatically by Google Play as part of standard Android Vitals for all apps distributed on the Play Store.
  • Provider: Google Play (Android Vitals), governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not add our own crash reporting service.
  • Retention: handled per Google Play's standard retention for Android Vitals data; if we add our own diagnostics tooling in the future, this policy will be updated first.

Locally Stored Data (On-Device Only)

  • Purpose: let you save favorite destinations and speed up repeat visits to market pages.
  • What's stored: your saved/favorited tourism destinations, and a temporary cache of market (pasar) listings and photos.
  • Storage: kept only in a local database on your device; never uploaded to our servers or any third party.
  • Control: remove individual favorites in-app, or clear all local data via Settings > Apps > JelajahDW > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.

How We Use Data

  • Provide routing, real-time navigation, simulation, and tour guidance.
  • Monitor and improve app reliability, performance, and user experience.

Legal Basis

We process data based on your consent (e.g., granting location permission) and our legitimate interests in providing a functional, secure service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share limited data with Mapbox (maps and routing) and Google Play services location (device location access), strictly to operate app features. We do not use our own crash reporting or analytics SDK; Google Play may separately collect crash/ANR data as part of standard Android Vitals.

Retention

  • Real-time location: not stored on our servers; used transiently for navigation requests to Mapbox.
  • Locally stored data (favorites, market cache): stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Crash/ANR diagnostics: collected and retained by Google Play under its own Android Vitals policy, not by us.

Children

JelajahDW is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect personal data from children under the age required by local law. If you are under that age, please use the app under parental guidance.

Security

  • Transport security (HTTPS) for network requests where applicable.
  • Least-privilege access controls for any internal tools and data.

Your Choices

  • Change or revoke app permissions in Settings > Apps > JelajahDW > Permissions.
  • Use Simulation mode to try routes without granting location.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all data collection.

International Transfers

Our third-party processors (Mapbox, Google Play services) may process data in other countries under their own safeguards and privacy policies.
